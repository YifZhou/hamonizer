# Source 7: Multilingual Performances and Cultural Displays

## Overview

Jiang Zemin was known for displaying his multilingual skills and cultural knowledge at diplomatic occasions. These performances are well-documented in multiple credible secondary sources and, in some cases, by video. This file collects verified instances.

---

## English

### Gettysburg Address Recitation

Jiang memorized and recited Abraham Lincoln's Gettysburg Address as a set piece for American visitors. He reportedly learned English partly by repeating major speeches.

**Verified instances:**
- During the 2000 Mike Wallace / *60 Minutes* interview: Jiang recited portions of the Gettysburg Address. Wallace used this as a segue to ask why Americans can elect their leaders but Chinese cannot. C-SPAN clip: https://www.c-span.org/clip/interview/user-clip-jiang-zemin-gettysburg-address/4813522
- Recited for various American dignitaries on multiple occasions.

*(Sources: Foreign Policy, April 9, 2012; ChinaFile, October 15, 2014; The China Project, November 30, 2022)*

---

## Italian

### "O Sole Mio"

Jiang sang the Neapolitan classic "O Sole Mio" in Italian on diplomatic occasions. The most documented instance:

- **2001:** When tenor Luciano Pavarotti visited Beijing, Jiang sang "O Sole Mio" with him on stage.

*(Sources: ChinaFile, October 15, 2014; The China Project, November 30, 2022; multiple press reports from the Pavarotti Beijing visit)*

**Song text (Italian original, standard version):**

> Che bella cosa na jurnata 'e sole,
> n'aria serena doppo na tempesta!
> Pe' ll'aria fresca pare già na festa...
> Che bella cosa na jurnata 'e sole.
>
> Ma n'atu sole cchiù bello, oi ne',
> 'o sole mio sta 'nfronte a te!
> 'O sole, 'o sole mio
> sta 'nfronte a te, sta 'nfronte a te!

*(Text: Giovanni Capurro, 1898; music: Eduardo di Capua, 1898. Standard Neapolitan lyrics; Jiang sang a standard rendition.)*

## Russian

### General Background

Jiang studied electrical engineering in the Soviet Union (Leningrad, 1955–56) and was proficient in Russian. He sang Russian songs on state visits to Russia and at informal occasions.

**Documented song:** 《莫斯科郊外的晚上》(Подмосковные вечера / "Moscow Nights")

Jiang sang this song during a 1997 state visit to Russia and at other Sino-Russian occasions. The song is associated with Sino-Soviet cultural exchange and was well-known to Chinese who studied in the USSR.

*(Sources: Multiple reports of Sino-Russian state visits; referenced in biographies. The specific video of Jiang singing this on the 1997 Russia visit has been mentioned in Chinese media.)*

**Song text (Russian original, first verse and chorus):**

> Не слышны в саду даже шорохи,
> Всё здесь замерло до утра.
> Если б знали вы, как мне дороги
> Подмосковные вечера.
>
> Речка движется и не движется,
> Вся из лунного серебра.
> Песня слышится и не слышится
> В эти тихие вечера.

*(Text: Mikhail Matusovsky, 1956; music: Vasily Solovyov-Sedoi, 1956)*

**Note:** Jiang's Russian-language singing is documented in biographical accounts and press reports but specific verified video clips are harder to pin to exact events compared to the Italian and English instances. Treat this as well-sourced but with slightly lower confidence than the "O Sole Mio" or Gettysburg Address instances.
