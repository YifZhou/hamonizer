# Jiang Zemin Quotes — Other Languages (Italian, Russian)

---

## Italian

### "O Sole Mio"

Jiang sang the Neapolitan song "O Sole Mio" on diplomatic occasions. The best-documented instance is his performance with tenor Luciano Pavarotti during Pavarotti's 2001 Beijing visit, where the two sang together on stage.

ChinaFile (2014) and multiple press reports document this as a characteristic Jiang performance.

**Standard text of "O Sole Mio" (Neapolitan, Giovanni Capurro / Eduardo di Capua, 1898):**

> Che bella cosa na jurnata 'e sole,
> n'aria serena doppo na tempesta!
> Pe' ll'aria fresca pare già na festa...
> Che bella cosa na jurnata 'e sole.
>
> Ma n'atu sole cchiù bello, oi ne',
> 'o sole mio sta 'nfronte a te!
> 'O sole, 'o sole mio
> sta 'nfronte a te, sta 'nfronte a te!

*(Jiang sang this in Italian. The specific verses he performed are not transcribed in the public record; the full standard text is provided for reference.)*

*(Source: ChinaFile, October 15, 2014; The China Project, November 30, 2022)*

## Russian

### 《莫斯科郊外的晚上》 / Подмосковные вечера (Moscow Nights)

Jiang studied in the Soviet Union (Leningrad, 1955–56) and retained Russian-language skills throughout his career. He sang Russian songs on state visits to Russia and at informal occasions. "Moscow Nights" is the most commonly cited.

*(Source: Biographical accounts; multiple reports of Sino-Russian summits in the 1990s–2000s. Video evidence exists but is not pinned to specific events in accessible archives.)*

**Standard text of "Moscow Nights," first verse and chorus (Russian, Mikhail Matusovsky / Vasily Solovyov-Sedoi, 1956):**

> Не слышны в саду даже шорохи,
> Всё здесь замерло до утра.
> Если б знали вы, как мне дороги
> Подмосковные вечера.
>
> Речка движется и не движется,
> Вся из лунного серебра.
> Песня слышится и не слышится
> В эти тихие вечера.

**Chinese version (for reference — the song was popularized in China under this title and text):**

> 深夜花园里四处静悄悄，
> 树叶也不再沙沙响。
> 夜色多么好，令我心神往，
> 在这迷人的晚上。
>
> 小河静静流，静静地流，
> 明月照水上，
> 啊，这美妙的夜晚，
> 在这迷人的晚上。

*(Chinese text: standard PRC translation of the song, widely known in China since the 1950s)*

## Note on Verification Standards

Language performances are documented in credible journalistic and biographical sources but are not transcribed in the way that press conferences are. The specific lyrics Jiang sang match the standard versions of these songs. The uncertainty is not in the text but in which specific verses he chose to perform on any given occasion.
