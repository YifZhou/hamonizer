# Jiang Zemin Quote Corpus

A curated, source-verified collection of quotes, speeches, and documented performances by Jiang Zemin (江泽民, 1926–2022), General Secretary of the CCP (1989–2002) and President of the PRC (1993–2003).

This corpus is assembled in support of a Claude skill for the 膜蛤 (Moha) internet subculture — a Chinese internet phenomenon built around ironic admiration of Jiang's persona.

---

## Background: Moha Culture (膜蛤文化)

"Moha" (膜蛤, literally "toad worship") is a Chinese internet meme culture that grew from Baidu Tieba around 2011–2014, reaching peak popularity during the 2014 Hong Kong protests. Participants, called 蛤丝 (toad fans) or 膜法师 (mogicians), adopt an ironic, affectionate stance toward Jiang's personality — his theatrical mannerisms, multilingual showmanship, and unguarded rhetorical outbursts.

The culture centers on three source videos, known as 蛤三篇 (Three Pieces About Toad):

| 篇 | Event | Date |
|----|-------|------|
| 篇一 | Rebuking Hong Kong journalists (张宝华事件) | October 27, 2000 |
| 篇二 | Mike Wallace / CBS 60 Minutes interview | September 3, 2000 (aired) |
| 篇三 | Visit to China United Engineering Corporation (国机二院) | April 23, 2009 |

## Verification Policy

All material in this corpus meets the following standard:

1. **Primary source identified:** Each quote is tied to a specific datable event with a named publication or broadcast as primary source.
2. **No fabrications:** Internet-circulated quotes without traceable primary sources are excluded.
3. **Original language preserved:** Quotes are stored in the language in which they were spoken. Interpreter-mediated quotes are labeled as such.
4. **Uncertainty flagged:** Where a quote is well-attested but not verbatim (e.g., some singing performances), this is stated explicitly.

### Common Internet Fabrications to Avoid

Many quotes circulate online falsely attributed to Jiang. The following are **not** in this corpus because they lack primary sourcing:
- Most "leaked" internal speech quotes
- Fabricated Sina Weibo posts attributed to Jiang
- Unverified quotes from "insider accounts" without named publication
