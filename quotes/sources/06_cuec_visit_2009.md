# Source 6: China United Engineering Corporation Visit (2009年视察国机二院)

## Metadata

- **Date:** April 23, 2009
- **Location:** China United Engineering Corporation (中国联合工程公司, formerly 国机二院 / China National Machinery Industry Corporation Second Design Institute), where Jiang had worked early in his career
- **Context:** Jiang, then retired, made a nostalgic return visit to the institution. He recalled his years working there, commented on the nation's development, and received a gift.
- **Languages:** Mandarin, English (one exclamation)
- **Moha significance:** This is "篇三" of the "蛤三篇" (Three Pieces About Toad). The word "Exciting!" (phonetically transliterated in Chinese internet culture as 亦可赛艇 / yìkě sàitǐng) originates here. The phrase "亦可赛艇" became a standalone Moha meme.
- **Source:** Original video circulated on Chinese social media platforms and Baidu Tieba. Referenced in multiple secondary sources including the Moha Wikipedia article and Fang (2020), *Information, Communication & Society*.

---

## Verified Content

### "Exciting!" / 亦可赛艇

When Jiang received a gift (reported variously as a model or commemorative item) from the institution, he exclaimed in English:

> **"Exciting!"**

(Some sources report "Excited!" — the ambiguity is real and debated. The phonetic transcription 亦可赛艇 matches "exciting" more closely than "excited.")

This English exclamation, embedded in an otherwise Chinese-language visit, became iconic. Chinese internet users rendered it as "亦可赛艇" (literally "also can sailboat"), a near-homophone that became a standalone internet meme unrelated to its original context.

---

### Calligraphic Inscription

During the visit, Jiang wrote a four-character inscription for the institution:

> **求真 务实 自强 创新**
> *(Seek truth, be pragmatic, strive for self-improvement, innovate)*

*(Source: Referenced in secondary accounts of the visit; the calligraphy was displayed at the institution.)*

---

### Reminiscences (General Content)

Jiang spoke about:
- His early career working at the institution as an engineer
- His personal experiences using a dip pen (鸭嘴笔), which he described as difficult to master
- Deng Xiaoping's decision to appoint him as the leader of China
- What he considered his achievements during his years of leadership

The specific text of these reminiscences has not been published in verbatim form in accessible Chinese-language official sources. The content is known primarily through the video footage and secondary reporting.

---

## Caution

The full verbatim transcript of this visit has not been published in official sources. The "Exciting!" exclamation is verified by video evidence; other attributed remarks from this visit that circulate online should be treated with caution unless sourced to the original footage.
