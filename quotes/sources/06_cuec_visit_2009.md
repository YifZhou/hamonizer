# Source 6: China United Engineering Corporation Visit (2009年视察国机二院)

## Metadata

- **Date:** April 23, 2009
- **Location:** China United Engineering Corporation (中国联合工程公司, formerly 国机二院), where Jiang worked as an engineer early in his career
- **Context:** Jiang, then retired, made a nostalgic return visit. He spoke at length about his engineering years, his unexpected political career, his accomplishments in office, and received a commemorative gift.
- **Languages:** Mandarin (with English insertions), Traditional/Simplified mixed (speaker used Mandarin; transcript reflects live speech including code-switches)
- **Moha significance:** 蛤三篇 篇三. Multiple Moha classics originate here: "Excited!", 微小的工作, 一个人的命运, 另请高明, 天堂的下面是你们的天堂.
- **Source:** Video footage circulated on Chinese social media and Baidu Tieba (~2014). Verbatim transcript preserved by 蛤丝 communities. Secondary citations: Fang (2020), *Information, Communication & Society*; English Wikipedia, Moha (meme).

### Engineering reminiscences

> 這個engineering drawing（工程作圖）呢，我們就有幾年用鴨嘴的筆，旁邊一個小盒子。最痛苦的，就是鴨嘴筆把這個水弄到裡面，描圖的時候一下子就……然後就用刀片刮，這個就是描圖最痛苦的，而且這個效率efficiency……

*(Jiang recalled the pain of using ruling pens (鸭嘴笔) in the pre-CAD era, code-switching to English for "engineering drawing" and "efficiency".)*

---

### Career trajectory: Shanghai to Beijing

> 我的這個经历就是到了上海，到了89年的年初的时候，我在想我估计是快要离休了，我想我应该去当教授。于是我就给朱物华校长、张钟俊院长，给他们写了一个报告。他们说欢迎你来，不过，这个Apply for Professor（申请当教授），你要去做一个报告。我就做了一个能源与发展趋势的主要的节能措施，这个报告经过好几百个教授一致通过。那么上海交大教授当了以后我就做第二个报告，就是微电子工业的发展。这两个报告做了以后不久，过后，1989年的5月31号北京就把我调到北京去了。

---

### On fate and self-struggle

> 人呐就都不知道，自己就不可以预料。一个人的命运啊，当然要靠自我奋斗，但是也要考虑到历史的行程。我绝对不知道，我作为一个上海市委书记怎么把我选到北京去了，所以邓小平同志跟我讲话，说"中央都决定啦，你来当总书记"，我说**另请高明**吧。我实在我也不是谦虚，我一个上海市委书记怎么到北京来了呢？但是呢，小平同志讲"大家已经研究决定了"，所以后来我就念了两首诗（原话如此），叫"**苟利国家生死以，岂因祸福避趋之**"，那麼所以我就到了北京。

---

### Three things / 微小的工作

> 到了北京我干了这十几年也没有什么别的，大概三件事：
>
> 一个，确立了社会主义市场经济；
>
> 第二个，把邓小平的理论列入了党章；
>
> 第三个，就是我們知道的"三个代表"。
>
> 如果说还有一点什麼成绩就是**军队一律不得经商**！这个对军队的命运有很大的关係。因為我后来又干了一年零八个月，等於我在部队干了15年军委主席。还有九八年的抗洪也是很大的。但这些都是次要的，我主要的我就是三件事情，**很惭愧，就做了一点微小的工作，谢谢大家。**

---

### Archive discovery

> 這是我用過的啊？老由啊，現在他們文印的圖章裡面絕對沒有這枚圖章，都不知道有這麼一個東西……這就是說明二院的檔案工作做得太好了！

*(Jiang reacted with delight to seeing an old official seal of his from his engineering days, preserved in the institution's archives.)*

---

### "Excited!" and 天堂的下面是你们的天堂

> （江泽民题字时）你们给我搞的这本东西啊，**Excited!**

> **天堂的下面是你们的天堂。**

*(Upon receiving the commemorative gift and writing the calligraphic inscription, Jiang exclaimed "Excited!" in English — the source of 亦可赛艇. The final line adapts the classical saying 上有天堂，下有苏杭 ("Above is heaven; below are Suzhou and Hangzhou") into a direct address to the engineers.)*

---

## Key Phrases and Moha Significance

| Quote | Moha use |
|---|---|
| **一个人的命运啊，当然要靠自我奋斗，但是也要考虑到历史的行程** | Philosophical meme; used to explain any improbable outcome as a blend of effort and historical forces |
| **另请高明** | False modesty when offered something important; ironic acceptance ritual |
| **苟利国家生死以，岂因祸福避趋之** | Principled duty invoked before accepting an unwanted burden; the Lin Zexu couplet as self-justification |
| **三件事 / 三个代表** | Three-part list structure; ironic bureaucratic grandeur |
| **军队一律不得经商** | The "bonus" achievement, delivered with outsized pride |
| **很惭愧，就做了一点微小的工作** | The defining understatement: three economy-scale achievements dismissed as 微小的工作 (tiny work) |
| **Excited!** / 亦可赛艇 | Enthusiasm expressed in English; phonetically memefied as 亦可赛艇 (also can sailboat) |
| **天堂的下面是你们的天堂** | Adapted classical phrase; Jiang addressing engineers with mock-cosmological grandeur |

## Calligraphic Inscription

During the visit, Jiang wrote a four-character inscription:

> **求真 务实 自强 创新**
> *(Seek truth, be pragmatic, strive for self-improvement, innovate)*

---

## Note on "Excited!" vs. "Exciting!"

The verbatim community transcript records **"Excited!"**. Earlier secondary sources and phonetic analysis of 亦可赛艇 suggested "Exciting!" The video transcript here resolves the ambiguity in favor of "Excited!" — though the phonetic rendering 亦可赛艇 remains the Moha canonical form regardless.
