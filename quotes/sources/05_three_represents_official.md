# Source 5: "Three Represents" Theory (三个代表)

## Metadata

- **First articulated:** February 25, 2000, during a symposium on party building in Guangzhou (广州)
- **Key speeches:** February 25, 2000; July 1, 2001 (CCP 80th Anniversary Rally); November 2002 (16th Party Congress)
- **Language:** Chinese (official); official English translation published by Foreign Languages Press

## The Three Represents (Original Chinese)

> 中国共产党始终代表中国先进生产力的发展要求，
> 始终代表中国先进文化的前进方向，
> 始终代表中国最广大人民的根本利益。

---

## Official English Translation

> The Communist Party of China always represents the development trends of China's advanced productive forces,
> always represents the orientations of China's advanced culture,
> and always represents the fundamental interests of the overwhelming majority of the people of China.

*(Source: Foreign Languages Press, Beijing, 2001)*

---

## From the 16th Party Congress Report (November 8, 2002)

This is the canonical policy speech in which Jiang formally enshrined the Three Represents into Party ideology. Selected passages:

> "It is essential to integrate the basic tenets of Marxism with the specific realities of contemporary China..."

> "We must act in the overall interests of the Chinese nation and work hard to blaze a new path of industrialization with high scientific and technological content, good economic returns, low resource consumption, little environmental pollution and a full display of the advantages of our human resources."

Full text: http://www.china.org.cn/english/2002/Nov/49107.htm

---

## Notes

- The Three Represents was incorporated into the CCP Constitution at the 16th Party Congress (2002) and into the PRC Constitution in 2004.
- It is sometimes mocked in Moha culture for its bureaucratic abstraction, but its text is officially verified and widely reproduced.
- Jiang's collected works on the topic: *Jiang Zemin wenxuan* (江泽民文选), People's Press, 2006, 3 vols.
