# Source 4: Barbara Walters / ABC 20/20 Interview (1990)

## Metadata

- **Date recorded:** May 2, 1990
- **Date aired:** May 18, 1990
- **Location:** Diaoyutai State Guesthouse (钓鱼台国宾馆), Beijing
- **Interviewer:** Barbara Walters, ABC News *20/20*
- **Language:** Jiang spoke in Chinese through interpreter; English quotes below are the interpreter's renderings as broadcast
- **Context:** This was one of Jiang's first major foreign media interviews as CCP General Secretary, less than a year after the June 4, 1989 Tiananmen Square crackdown. He had been appointed by Deng Xiaoping on June 24, 1989, days after the massacre.
- **Primary source:** ABC News *20/20*, aired May 18, 1990. Documented in CECC (Congressional-Executive Commission on China) hearing testimony; archived at "Standoff at Tiananmen" blog (citing the original broadcast). YouTube clip of the Tank Man portion: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Gumn6z0RlaE

---

## Verified Quotes

### On the 1989 Tiananmen Crackdown

Jiang Zemin described the military suppression of the 1989 pro-democracy movement as:

> **"Much ado about nothing."**

He stated that the strong reactions by Western countries to the crackdown were "much ado about nothing," and maintained that the government's actions had been necessary.

*(Source: Standoff at Tiananmen, "This Day in 1989: May 18, 1990, Jiang Zemin Interviewed by Barbara Walters," http://www.standoffattiananmen.com/2017/08/this-day-in-1989-may-18-1990-jiang.html; cited in CECC hearing document by Jeffrey Wasserstrom; TIME magazine.)*

---

## Notes on Verification

- The phrase "much ado about nothing" is widely attributed to this interview in academic and journalistic sources, including:
  - Jeffrey Wasserstrom's written testimony to the CECC (Congressional-Executive Commission on China): https://www.cecc.gov/sites/evo-subsites/cecc.house.gov/files/CECC%20Hearing%20-%20Tiananmen%20-%20Jeffrey%20Wasserstrom%20Written%20Statement.pdf
  - TIME magazine archive
  - Isaac Stone Fish, *Foreign Policy*, April 9, 2012: "He told Barbara Walters in 1990 that the 1989 Tiananmen Massacre was 'much ado about nothing.'"
- The quote is given in English (interpreter's rendering); the original Chinese phrasing as spoken by Jiang is not in the public record.
- A YouTube clip showing the portion about the Tank Man is available but does not contain the "much ado about nothing" segment verbatim in accessible form.
